The Seven Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey in two minutes.

Most people live their entire lives in reaction mode. They blame their boss, the economy, or the weather, feeling completely powerless. But here is the brutal truth. If you want to change your life, you have to work from the inside out.

Habit One. Be proactive. Unsuccessful people obsess over their Circle of Concern, worrying about things they cannot control. Highly effective people pour all their energy into their Circle of Influence. You are not a product of your circumstances. You are a product of your decisions.

Habit Two. Begin with the end in mind. Imagine attending your own funeral three years from now. What do you want your family and friends to say about your character? If your ladder is leaning against the wrong wall, every single step you take just gets you to the wrong place faster.

Habit Three. Put first things first. Most people spend their days putting out urgent fires. But urgent rarely means important. You must schedule your big rocks first. Your deep work, your health, and your family, before the small pebbles and sand consume your day.

Habit Four. Think win win. Life is not a zero sum game where someone must lose for you to win. Scarcity mindset creates bitter enemies. An abundance mindset builds lasting empires.

Habit Five. Seek first to understand, then to be understood. Most people do not listen to understand. They listen with the intent to reply. Diagnose before you prescribe.

Habit Six. Synergize. One plus one does not equal two. When two creative minds combine, one plus one equals three, five, or ten. Value the differences.

Habit Seven. Sharpen the saw. A lumberjack spent five hours furiously trying to chop down a tree with a blunt blade. When asked to stop and sharpen his saw, he said, I do not have time, I am too busy sawing. If you never recharge your physical, mental, and spiritual energy, you will inevitably burn out.
