The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason in two minutes.

Four thousand years ago, Babylon was the wealthiest city on Earth. Its streets were paved with gold, yet most citizens lived in desperate poverty. Why? Because they spent every single coin they earned. Here is the timeless secret of Arkad, the richest man in Babylon.

First, start thy purse to fattening. Pay yourself first. For every ten coins you earn, spend only nine. If you pay the merchant, the landlord, and the tailor first, you are a financial slave working for everyone except yourself. That tenth coin is yours to keep, forever.

Second, control thy expenditures. Do not confuse necessary expenses with your desires. What you call necessities will always grow to equal your income unless you set clear boundaries. Budget your expenses so you have gold to pay yourself, without depriving your life.

Third, make thy gold multiply. A hoarded coin is dead metal. Gold is a willing worker. Put every coin to work so its golden children and grandchildren earn income for you while you sleep. Wealth is not the coins in your pocket. It is the steady stream of income flowing continuously into your purse.

Fourth, guard thy treasures from loss. The first sound principle of investment is security of your principal. Never trust get-rich-quick schemes, and never ask a bricklayer for advice on buying diamonds. Consult only wise men who have proven experience managing gold.

Fifth, make of thy dwelling a profitable investment. Own your own roof so your heart is proud and your living costs are reduced.

Sixth, insure a future income. Provide in advance for your old age and protect your family against sudden misfortune.

And seventh, increase thy ability to earn. The more wisdom you acquire, the more gold you will attract. Cultivate your skills, respect yourself, and seize opportunities without hesitation. Gold flees the man who gambles, but clings to the man who follows these timeless laws.
